My Top 5 Films

Welcome! This site is my personal ranking of five films I love, along with a look at how my own ratings compare with the scores other viewers give them on IMDb. I chose a mix of eras, countries, and genres on purpose: a Hollywood swashbuckler from 1940, an Oscar-winning Soviet comedy-drama, a Brooklyn romantic comedy, a gritty crime drama from 1990s Russia, and a satirical sci-fi heist film from 2026. Together they show how many different things a movie can be, and why I keep coming back to all of them.

When I rank films, I try to ask the same few questions each time. Do the characters feel alive? Does the story stay with me after the credits roll? Would I happily watch it again? A film does not need to be famous or perfectly made to score well with me. Sometimes the ones that take a big risk, or that capture a particular time and place, end up higher on my list than the more polished ones.

IMDb averages the ratings of hundreds of thousands of viewers, so it makes a useful point of comparison. On the Films page you will find a chart that places my score for each film beside its IMDb score, so you can see where we agree and where we do not. Several of these movies are older or come from outside Hollywood, so they may be new to you. If you have never seen a Soviet comedy-drama or a 1940s adventure, I hope the short descriptions there convince you to try one. And if you think I ranked something in the wrong place, I would love to hear your argument.
